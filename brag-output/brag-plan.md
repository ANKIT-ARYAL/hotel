# Brag Plan: Hotel Luxury

## What is this app?

Hotel Luxury is a database-driven hospitality website where guests discover rooms and services, authenticate with Google, make room/spa/dining/experience requests, and manage their stay from a guest dashboard while hotel staff manage operations from an admin and reception portal.

## The angle

A quiet luxury hotel experience that turns a beautiful public website into a real operational system: discover a stay, request a service, then watch the request land in the hotel team’s workflow.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)

Open on the pool hero and the line “Where Elegance Meets Exceptional Service,” then snap into the guest account dashboard so the video immediately proves this is more than a brochure site.

## Key moments (the middle)

- Guest dashboard with room bookings, spa requests, dining requests, and experience bookings.
- Experience page with a treatment-style “Book experience” action and a database-backed reservation form.
- Staff operations view receiving and managing the experience reservation with guest, time, party size, notes, and status.

## Outro / punchline

Return to the HOTEL LUXURY logo with: “Beautiful for guests. Ready for the front desk.”

## User flow worth showing

Public experience page → guest selects an experience and submits a reservation request → staff confirms it in Experience Reservations.

## Tone

- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet luxury meets real hotel operations
- Interpretation: restrained editorial motion, warm typography, cinematic hospitality imagery, and crisp UI transitions.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)

- Background: warm ivory / off-white with subtle grain
- Accent: near-black charcoal and muted gray
- Text: charcoal and white over imagery
- Display font: Cormorant Garamond
- Logo font: Syne
- Strongest visual element: full-bleed pool hero followed by the guest account cards and operations reservation card.

## Share copy (draft)

Hotel Luxury connects the guest journey and the front desk in one polished flow—from discovering an experience to confirming it.

## Audio direction

- Role: cinematic support
- Music: restrained elegant hospitality bed
- Music treatment: soft opening swell, gentle lift during the booking action, clean resolution on the logo.
- Music cue guidance: detect cues at composition time; target the hero reveal, form submission, and staff confirmation.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let transitions breathe with the music rather than adding waveform effects.
- SFX posture: sparse and polished; use quiet interface taps for selection and confirmation.
- Audio-coupled moments: experience selection, form submit, and confirmation status.
- Restraint rule: no loud notification sounds or generic startup effects.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Arrival — 3s

Full-bleed pool hero with the actual HOTEL LUXURY logo and “Where Elegance Meets Exceptional Service.”

Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: cinematic opening swell
Audio-coupled idea: logo and headline settle on the swell
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Choose the moment — 4s

Experience cards appear with real experience names and the “Book experience” action.

Sequential/interaction: cards reveal one by one, then a simulated tap opens the reservation form
Audio intent: elegant forward motion
Audio-coupled idea: subtle tap on the form opening
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Make it personal — 4s

The form shows the selected experience, date/time, guest count, and notes before submission.

Sequential/interaction: selected experience and form fields appear in order
Audio intent: focused, intimate
Audio-coupled idea: light field-selection ticks
Transition mood: smooth wipe → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Guest account — 4s

Guest dashboard shows bookings, spa requests, dining requests, and experience bookings as separate cards.

Sequential/interaction: experience booking card receives a pending status
Audio intent: reassuring confirmation
Audio-coupled idea: quiet status tick
Transition mood: slide → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Ready for the front desk — 5s

Admin Experience Reservations card shows the guest, selected experience, scheduled time, party size, notes, and Confirm action. Finish on HOTEL LUXURY.

Sequential/interaction: Confirm action changes the status to confirmed
Audio intent: confident resolution
Audio-coupled idea: restrained confirmation accent and final logo hit
Transition mood: cinematic crossfade

**Music mood for this video:** cinematic
**Audio summary:** a restrained hospitality bed grows from discovery to reservation confirmation, then resolves cleanly on the logo.
